# Transformers

## The original encoder–decoder Transformer

[Watch or download the complete lesson](transformer-complete-lesson.mp4)

A 14-minute, 19-second conceptual lesson based on *Attention Is All You Need* (Vaswani et al., 2017), with synthetic English narration, illustrated diagrams, captions, and chapter markers.

Topics: tokenization and embeddings; positional encoding; queries, keys, and values; attention weights and multi-head attention; feed-forward layers; residual connections and normalization; masked decoder attention and cross-attention; final output generation.

Each topic begins with a child-friendly analogy, followed by conceptual mathematics. Examples are teaching illustrations.
